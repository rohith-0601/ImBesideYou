### 2.3 Boundary alignment

- **episode** boundaries (process switches): **144 / 150 (96.0%)** within 5.0s of a `browser_navigation` or `app_switch`
- **execution** boundaries (individual cases): **440 / 586 (75.1%)**

> Caveat, stated because it matters: the episode figure is close to tautological. Episodes are cut where the process context changes, and the context changes *because* of navigation events, so a high score confirms the implementation does what it intends — not that the boundaries are the ones a human would draw. It is a regression guard, not accuracy.

> The execution figure is *expected* to be much lower, and that is the point: most case boundaries happen inside one screen, with no navigation to align with. A high number there would mean the executions were not being split at all.
