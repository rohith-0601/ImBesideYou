### 2.3 Boundary alignment

**146 / 147 (99.3%)** segment starts sit within 5.0s of a `browser_navigation` or `app_switch`.

> Caveat, stated because it matters: this check is close to tautological. Segments are cut where the process context changes, and the context changes *because* of navigation events, so a high score here confirms the implementation does what it intends — not that the boundaries are the ones a human would draw. It is reported as a regression guard, not as accuracy.
